ACADEMY PILLARS

DEVELOPMENT STARTS WITH
THE RIGHT FOUNDATION.

Providing young footballers in Kolkata with structured training, physical conditioning, tactical awareness, and sportsmanship.

PILLAR 01

PROFESSIONAL FOOTBALL TRAINING

Structured training sessions engineered to teach young players proper body mechanics, pitch discipline, positional responsibility, and modern football fundamentals.

Every session is designed to instill a high work ethic, concentration, and focus on continuous improvement.

PILLAR 02

FITNESS, AGILITY & COORDINATION

Physical conditioning tailored for football agility, explosive acceleration, stamina, core stability, and dynamic balance.

Helping young athletes build injury resistance, movement efficiency, and physical resilience.

PILLAR 03

TECHNICAL SKILLS & BALL CONTROL

Focused drills targeting first-touch control, two-footed passing accuracy, dribbling tight maneuvers, and striking technique under pressure.

Developing players who are composed, confident, and technical with the ball at their feet.

PILLAR 04

MATCH AWARENESS & TACTICAL UNDERSTANDING

Teaching young players to read the game, anticipate plays, maintain defensive spacing, and make fast tactical decisions during match scenarios.

Building intelligent footballers with acute spatial awareness and tactical comprehension.

TEAMWORK & DISCIPLINE

Building strong team synergy, communication, punctuality, and mutual respect among squad members.

CONFIDENCE & SPORTSMANSHIP

Nurturing athletic confidence, humility in victory, resilience in defeat, and sportsmanship toward opponents and referees.
