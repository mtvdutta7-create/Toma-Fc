ABOUT OUR ACADEMY

MORE THAN TRAINING.
IT'S A JOURNEY.

Toma FC Rabindra Nagar East is a football academy dedicated to developing young footballers through structured training, discipline, teamwork, and a strong passion for the game.

Our aim is to provide players with a positive and supportive environment where they can improve their football skills, physical fitness, confidence, game awareness, and sportsmanship.

KNOW MORE →

OUR MISSION

To help young players develop their football potential while learning the values of discipline, respect, teamwork, and dedication.

OUR VISION

To develop talented and disciplined footballers and create opportunities for young players to progress in the sport.
