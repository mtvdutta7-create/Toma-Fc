OUR CLUB PROFILE

POSITIVE & SUPPORTIVE ENVIRONMENT

Toma FC Rabindra Nagar East was established in 2026 in Kolkata, West Bengal, with a clear focus: empowering young footballers to achieve their highest athletic potential.

Our aim is to provide players with a positive and supportive environment where they can improve their football skills, physical fitness, confidence, game awareness, and sportsmanship.

Through methodical coaching, players learn that true success comes from relentless effort, discipline, and mutual support.
